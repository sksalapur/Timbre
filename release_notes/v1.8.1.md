# 🎵 Timbre v1.8.1: Upstream Synchronization, Custom Glass Icons & Audio Continuity

Welcome to **Timbre v1.8.1**! This release synchronizes with upstream **BitChord v1.8.1** (by Kushagra Singh), introduces dynamic **Glass App Icon switching**, fixes the tail-end audio stutter on streaming tracks, adds direct **Google Cookie Login & Full Auth Backup/Restore**, and strengthens our bot infrastructure.

---

### 🌟 Release Highlights & What's New

#### 1. 🎨 Dynamic App Icon Customization
- **12 Custom Glass Icons**: Choose between 3 iconic form factors across 4 glass styles directly from **Settings ➔ Advanced Options ➔ App Icon**:
  - **Liquid Glass** (Standard, Dark, Clear, Clear Dark)
  - **Samsung Glass** (Standard, Dark, Clear, Clear Dark)
  - **Pixel Glass** (Standard, Dark, Clear, Clear Dark)
  - **Default** adaptive app icon
- Centered high-resolution cyan-and-white Timbre wave insignia with specular glass reflections and ambient neon glows.

#### 2. 🎧 Audio Stream Continuity ("Missed Beat" Stutter Fix)
- Resolved an audio stutter glitch that occurred during the last 5–10 seconds of streaming tracks.
- When ExoPlayer requested tail buffer chunks, continuation requests previously invalidated the stream choice mid-stream. Continuation requests (`position > 0`) now stream uninterrupted from cache.

#### 3. 🔐 Session & Cookie Backup/Restore + Direct Cookie Login
- **Full Auth Portability**: Timbre's JSON Backup & Restore now backs up your YouTube Music session cookies, channel profile IDs, and Discord tokens.
- **Direct Cookie Login**: Added "Log in with Cookie" to **Settings ➔ Account & Scrobbling**, allowing instant login via raw session cookies without opening the WebView.

#### 4. 🎛️ Timbre Studio & Audiophile Pipeline (Preserved & Enhanced)
- Retained Timbre's signature **Bit-Perfect / Hi-Res Lossless** pipeline badge and real-time **Signal Path Inspector**.
- **Exclusive USB DAC Userspace Streaming**: Direct bit-exact playback bypassing Android's 48kHz AudioFlinger OS mixer.
- **24-bit Float32 librempeg Decoder**: Pristine studio master decoding without 16-bit truncation.

#### 5. 🤖 Bot & Gateway Infrastructure Hardening
- Reclaimed and re-secured `@Timbre_Lossless_Bot`.
- Added a strict private chat-type guard so the bot exclusively operates in 1-on-1 direct messages and ignores public group messages.
