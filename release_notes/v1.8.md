## 💎 Timbre Audiophile Architecture
- **Bit-Perfect Lossless Pipeline**: Uncompressed 24-bit/192kHz direct hardware output to external USB DACs and Type-C DSP earphones (such as Moondrop Chu II DSP at native 96kHz).
- **Lossless-First Audio Routing (0:00)**: Studio FLAC sources are prioritized from the first second with a customizable **0.5s – 5.0s Lossless Priority Window** in *Settings → Playback*.
- **Predictive 4-Track Lookahead**: Quadrupled queue lookahead pre-caching from 1 track to 4 tracks (2-second stagger), ensuring instant gapless transitions.
- **Accurate Decoded PCM Telemetry**: Nerd Stats displays live physical decoded PCM throughput (e.g., 1,411 kbps for 16/44.1, 4,608 kbps for 24/96) matching hardware sink throughput.
- **Timbre Waveform Vector Identity**: Redesigned minimalist audio waveform vector branding across app headers, status-bar notifications, and Replay cards.
- **Streamlined Mobile Performance**: Stripped desktop/TV overhead to maximize Android battery efficiency and RAM throughput.

- **Playlist Management**: Reorder songs inside custom playlists, bulk-add tracks, and view total album/playlist duration.
- **Listen Together Enhancements**: Invite deep-linking and synchronized queue state across devices.

## 🏛️ Upstream Foundation & Other Versions
Timbre is a specialized Android mobile audiophile fork built upon [**BitChord**](https://github.com/kushagrasinghx/BitChord) by [**Kushagra Singh**](https://github.com/kushagrasinghx).

- **Desktop & TV Builds**: If you need Windows, Linux, macOS, Android TV, or Fire TV builds, please download upstream [BitChord Releases](https://github.com/kushagrasinghx/BitChord/releases).
- **Version Lineage**:
  - **v1.8**: Spotify library integration, redesigned artist credits, frosted glass action menus, playlist management.
  - **v1.7**: Revamped playback pipeline, high-precision lyric clock sync, and enhanced network stream caching.
  - **v1.6**: Listen Together group sessions, WebDAV & SMB custom storage support, and Discord RPC scrobbling.
  - **v1.5 & earlier**: Core architecture, YouTube Music scraping engine, and initial multiplatform foundation.
