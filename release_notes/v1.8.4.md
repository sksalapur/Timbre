# 💎 Timbre v1.8.4: Full Upstream Sync, Audiophile Bluetooth Truth, Artwork Preload & Priority Resolution

Welcome to **Timbre v1.8.4**! This major release fully synchronizes with upstream **BitChord v1.8.1** (by Kushagra Singh) bringing across all 176 upstream commits, introduces the brand new **Welcome & Onboarding experience**, dynamic **Glass App Icon switching**, fixes tail-end audio stream stutter, enforces **Technical Truth for Bluetooth Audio & Anti-Crash Telemetry**, guarantees **Lossless Priority Resolution on Rapid Skip**, introduces **Queue Lookahead Artwork Pre-Warming (0ms cover art)**, adds **Seamless Auth & Source Backup & Restore**, and strengthens our bot infrastructure.

---

### 🚀 Release Highlights & What's New

#### 1. 🔄 Full Upstream BitChord v1.8.1 Synchronization
- **New Onboarding & Welcome Screen**: Clean, interactive setup experience on fresh installations:
  - Personalize your Listen Together profile name (*"What should we call you?"*).
  - Quick, seamless Google account connection with YouTube Music or option to skip.
- **Search Swipe Actions**: Swipe track items in search history to instantly append or prepend them to the playing queue.
- **Queue History & Replay Stats**: Optional queue history display and accurate weekly/monthly/yearly Listening Stats with daily granularity.
- **Wear OS Companion**: Comprehensive companion management for connected Wear OS watches directly inside Settings.
- **Song Info**: Detailed track metadata inspector (Album, Release Date, Views, Likes, and Comments).
- **RTL & Synchronized Lyrics**: Complete bidirectional RTL text layout and fluid word-sweep karaoke animations across all language combinations.

#### 2. 🔷 Truthful Bluetooth Hi-Res Audio & Anti-Crash Architecture
- **Technical Truth for Bluetooth Transmission**:
  - Bluetooth A2DP audio (including LDAC 990 kbps and LHDCv5 1000 kbps) is routed through Android's `AudioFlinger` mixer and undergoes lossy psychoacoustic compression.
  - **Emerald Green (`#00E676`)** is strictly preserved for true **Bit-Perfect Direct Hardware DACs** (USB DACs with direct hardware bypass).
  - **Electric Cyan (`#00E5FF`)** designates **Hi-Res Wireless (`🔷 HI-RES WIRELESS (LHDCv5 / LDAC)`)**, explicitly stating `(Lossy Bluetooth transmission via AudioFlinger)` and `No — Bluetooth LHDCv5 encoding` in the bit-exact verdict.
- **Eliminated Infinite Crash Loop**:
  - Fixed a re-entrant feedback loop where `applyOutputRoute()` in `PlaybackService` was invoking `bluetoothTracker.refreshCurrentDevice()`, which published a new telemetry state triggering `applyOutputRoute()`. This flooded Android's message queue and crashed the app during Bluetooth route changes.
- **Instant Active Codec Detection**:
  - Added immediate active device and codec reflection (`queryAudioManagerFallback`) so that connected Hi-Res earbuds (e.g. realme Buds T500 Pro -> LHDCv5 24-bit / 96 kHz) are identified immediately on app launch, route switch, or opening the pipeline dialog—without requiring earbud disconnect/reconnect.

#### 3. 🎯 Strict Lossless Priority Resolution Window on Rapid Skip
- **No More Premature Lossy Fallbacks**:
  - Rapidly skipping tracks now strictly honors your configured Lossless Priority Resolution Window (`losslessTimeoutSeconds`).
- **Clean Track Handover**:
  - Stale lossy choices are automatically cleared when skipping to track position 0, guaranteeing that FLAC modules (Tidal, Qobuz, Lossless Bridge) are given full time to resolve before any lossy YouTube fallback can trigger.

#### 4. 🖼️ Queue Lookahead Artwork Pre-Warming (Instant 0ms Cover Art)
- **Instant Cover Art on Track Switch**:
  - Eliminates the 2–3s delay in album art rendering when skipping tracks.
  - Implements sequential, bounded queue artwork prefetching for up to 4 upcoming tracks at full resolution (`PLAYER_ART_PX = 1200`).
- **100% Network Priority for Hi-Res Audio**:
  - Defers artwork downloads by 2.5s after playback starts and paces requests sequentially with 300ms spacing, ensuring that FLAC stream resolution and audio buffering enjoy completely uncontended bandwidth.
  - Subsequent track skips render artwork in **0ms** from local cache.

#### 5. 🎨 Dynamic App Icon Customization
- **12 Custom Glass Icons**: Choose between 3 iconic form factors across 4 glass styles directly from **Settings → Appearance → App Icon**:
  - **Liquid Glass** (Standard, Dark, Clear, Clear Dark)
  - **Samsung Glass** (Standard, Dark, Clear, Clear Dark)
  - **Pixel Glass** (Standard, Dark, Clear, Clear Dark)
  - **Default** adaptive app icon
- Centered high-resolution cyan-and-white Timbre wave insignia with specular glass reflections and ambient neon glows.

#### 6. ⚡ Audio Stream Continuity ("Missed Beat" Stutter Fix)
- Resolved an audio stutter glitch that occurred during the last 5–10 seconds of streaming tracks.
- When ExoPlayer requested tail buffer chunks, continuation requests previously invalidated the stream choice mid-stream. Continuation requests (`position > 0`) now stream uninterrupted from cache.

#### 7. 💾 Seamless Auth & Source Backup & Restore
- **Full Auth Portability**: Timbre's JSON Backup & Restore engine (Settings → Backup & Restore) now serializes your YouTube Music session cookies, channel profile IDs, Discord tokens, custom streaming sources, and addon tokens.
- **Zero-Friction Restore**: Importing a backup automatically activates your logged-in Google session without requiring manual cookie entry.

#### 8. 🎚️ Timbre Studio & Audiophile Pipeline (Preserved & Enhanced)
- Retained Timbre's signature **Bit-Perfect / Hi-Res Lossless** pipeline badge and real-time **Signal Path Inspector**.
- **Exclusive USB DAC Userspace Streaming**: Direct bit-exact playback bypassing Android's 48kHz AudioFlinger OS mixer.
- **24-bit Float32 librempeg Decoder**: Pristine studio master decoding without 16-bit truncation.

#### 9. 🛡️ Bot & Gateway Infrastructure Hardening
- Reclaimed and re-secured `@Timbre_Lossless_Bot` with fresh API credentials.
- Added a strict private chat-type guard so the bot exclusively operates in 1-on-1 direct messages and ignores public group messages.
