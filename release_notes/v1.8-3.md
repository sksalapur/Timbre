# 🎧 Timbre v1.8-3: The True Bit-Perfect & USB DAC Breakthrough

Welcome to **Timbre v1.8-3**, our most significant audiophile engineering milestone to date. This major release delivers **true Exclusive Userspace USB DAC Access**, completely bypassing Android's 48 kHz AudioFlinger system mixer and kernel ALSA drivers, resolves the 24-bit to 16-bit software decoder bottleneck, ensures seamless bit-exact playback across track transitions, and introduces full **Timbre Studio** backup/restore integration.

---

### 🚀 Major Breakthroughs & Highlights in v1.8-3

#### 1. ⚡ Full 24-bit Hi-Res Signal Path (Eliminating the 16-bit Decoder Bottleneck)
* **FFmpeg / libavcodec Priority**: Previously, Android's platform software decoder (`c2.android.flac.decoder`) intercepted FLAC streams and downconverted 24-bit audio to 16-bit integer PCM before reaching the audio sink.
* **Native Float32 Decoding**: We now prioritize `FfmpegAudioRenderer` (`librempeg`), natively decoding 24-bit and 32-bit FLAC/ALAC into pristine 32-bit floating-point PCM (`ENCODING_PCM_FLOAT`).
* **Untouched Dynamic Range**: 24-bit / 44.1 kHz, 48 kHz, 96 kHz, and 192 kHz studio masters now travel unquantized from network edge to DAC hardware without bit-depth loss.

#### 2. 🔌 True Exclusive USB DAC Access (Bypassing AudioFlinger)
* **Direct Userspace USB Streaming**: Communicates directly with external USB Audio Class devices (both UAC1 and UAC2, including DSP IEMs like Moondrop Chu II DSP, Fiio, Qudelix, and desktop DACs) via Android's USB Host API.
* **Kernel & Framework Bypass**: Completely bypasses the Linux kernel `snd-usb-audio` driver and Android's fixed 48 kHz AudioFlinger mixer, eliminating unwanted OS resampling, dither noise, and digital attenuation.
* **Native Sample Rate Delivery**: Play 44.1 kHz CD audio, 96 kHz, and 192 kHz Hi-Res files at their exact native hardware clocks.

#### 3. 🛡️ Smart Gated USB Permission & Hot-Plug Lifecycle
* **Zero Annoyance Gating**: The app now requests Exclusive USB Access **only when the "Direct Bit-Perfect Mode" toggle is turned ON** in Settings. If the toggle is off, no permission popups will ever appear.
* **Dynamic Toggle Activation**: If your USB DAC is already plugged in and you flip the Bit-Perfect toggle ON, Timbre detects it immediately and prompts for authorization on the spot.
* **Seamless Hot-Reconnection**: Disconnecting and reconnecting your USB DAC automatically prompts for authorization and immediately triggers a forced pipeline rebuild, restoring green bit-perfect output instantly without needing manual setting toggles.
* **Foreground-Only Lifecycle**: Observers strictly register and unregister with Activity foreground states (`onStart` to `onStop`), ensuring background operations are never disrupted.

#### 4. 🎵 Gapless Crossfade & Track-Transition Bit-Exact Fix
* **Eliminated False "Converted Downstream" on Song Change**: Resolved an architectural dual-player crossfade bug where natural track transitions dropped telemetry, leaving the status as "Bit-exact: No (converted downstream)".
* **Audible Buffer Certification**: `PrecisionAudioSink` now dynamically certifies bit-exact telemetry upon active buffer emission, ensuring continuous 🟢 BIT-PERFECT: Bit-Exact Signal Path certification across your entire playlist.

#### 5. 🔍 Truthful Color-Coding & Signal Path Certification
* **Strict Certification**: The Audio Pipeline inspector now enforces honest color-coding. The badge will **never** display green if audio is routed through AudioFlinger, downsampled, or routed to the built-in speaker.
* **Truthful Yellow Badges**:
  * 🟨 `Framework Mixed (AudioFlinger)`: Clearly indicates when audio is managed by the OS mixer.
  * 🟨 `Downsampled (24-bit → 16-bit)`: Alerts you if output precision is restricted.
* **Phone Speaker Recognition & Guidance**:
  * Phone speakers are identified as 📢 `Phone Speaker (Framework Mixed)`.
  * Informational banners explain that built-in speakers always pass through AudioFlinger and OEM protection limiters, with direct guidance: *"Connect an IEM or a lossless-codec-supported TWS (e.g. LDAC / LHDC) for bit-perfect audio."*
* **Detailed USB Hardware Inspector**: Displays live USB Protocol (UAC1/UAC2), Exclusive Access state, DAC Max Supported Sample Rates, Supported Bit Depths, and Kernel Driver status.

#### 6. 💾 Timbre Studio Settings & Add-ons in Backup & Restore
* **Complete Data Portability**: Timbre's JSON Backup & Restore engine (`Settings` → `Backup & Restore`) now fully serializes:
  * All Custom Streaming Sources and Community Add-ons.
  * Custom Source priority rankings and health parameters.
  * All Timbre Studio configuration preferences and DAC options.
* Seamlessly migrate your entire bespoke audiophile setup across devices in one click.

#### 7. 🌐 Source Edge Health & Persistent FLAC Resolution (from v1.8-2)
* **Interactive Multi-Provider Health Dialog**: Live ping and latency testing across Lossless Bridge, Timbre Cloudflare Edge, YouTube Music, and JioSaavn with HTTP/2 persistent connection pooling (<50ms re-test latency).
* **Disk-Backed StreamChoice**: Resolved FLAC stream URLs persist across cold app restarts with a 12-hour validity window.
* **Contaminated Cache Purge**: Automatically detects and purges lossy Opus bytes in HiFi cache slots before playback begins.
