Engineered for Audiophiles

TrueBit transforms everyday mobile streaming into a zero-compromise high-resolution listening experience.

🎵

Bit-Perfect Audio Engine

Engineered for uncompressed high-resolution playback with custom audio addon compatibility, bit-perfect direct output, and an active audiophile Telegram community.

🎯

Configurable Priority Window

Stock players default to lossy Opus and clumsily upgrade mid-track. TrueBit queries lossless studio sources first. With the newly introduced 0.5s – 5.0s Playback Slider, you decide the exact safety window before falling back.

🎛️

Direct Bit-Perfect Hardware Sink

Android's default system audio mixer aggressively resamples all audio to 48kHz and truncates bit depths. TrueBit leverages AndroidX Media3's direct AudioTrack pipeline, driving USB DACs and Type-C DSPs (such as Moondrop Chu II DSP) at native 96 kHz.

⏩

4-Track Predictive Lookahead

Skip without delays. TrueBit increases background queue lookahead from 1 to 4 tracks and optimizes prefetch intervals to 2 seconds. Upcoming album tracks are pre-resolved and primed in memory before you tap Next.

📊

Accurate Bitrate Telemetry

Eliminated upstream telemetry leaks. TrueBit's Nerd Stats accurately displays genuine decoded PCM data rates (e.g. 1,411 kbps for 16/44.1, 4,608 kbps for 24/96) matching your hardware sink's physical throughput.

🎨

Aesthetic Modern Player

Enjoy Spotify playlist integration, character-synchronized lyrics powered by lrc.red, beat-matched Automix, high-resolution covers, and a fluid Material 3 Expressive UI with glassmorphic accents.
